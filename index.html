Delivery Leadership Approach (PRISM)

My delivery leadership approach follows a PRISM model—designed to drive predictable, scalable, and intelligence-led delivery outcomes across global programs.

  • P — Predictability: Strong governance, execution discipline, and milestone control to reduce delivery risk and surprises.
  • R — Results: Clear alignment of delivery outcomes with customer value, CSAT, and revenue realization.
  • I — Intelligence: Pragmatic use of AI to enhance visibility, early risk identification, trend analysis, and decision-making.
  • S — Scale: Proven ability to operate and stabilize multi-region, multi-vendor delivery environments.
  • M — Metrics: Outcome-driven KPIs and reporting focused on business impact rather than activity tracking.
